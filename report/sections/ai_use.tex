\section*{Use of AI}

\subsection*{Tools}
I used Claude (Anthropic) through Claude Code.
Claude Fable~5.1 retrieved the data and found possible accounting issues. I ran it with the \texttt{/goal} command.
Claude Opus~5.5 wrote the first draft of the report.
I used \url{https://impeccable.style/} to improve the layout, the graphs and the readability.
I used \LaTeX{} because it is easy to use with AI.
Python did all the calculations. Thus, you can verify each number.

\subsection*{What AI did}
Claude downloaded the LVMH and Diageo reports and converted them to Markdown.
It found the figures in the notes. A script checked each figure against the cited PDF page.
It calculated the implied values with Python. It also made the tables, figures and journal entries.
AI explained the issue to me and showed me the applicable standards and documents.
I also made a video with AI to explain the issue.
For the draft, the ASD-STE100 standard (Simplified Technical English) gave good results. A post by Andrej Karpathy suggested this method: \url{https://x.com/karpathy/status/2105819303471976479}.

\subsection*{Limitations}
AI often states causal links that nobody verified, or links that are too general.
Also, AI output is frequently difficult for people to read and understand.
Thus, people must decide what a report contains and what it does not contain.
